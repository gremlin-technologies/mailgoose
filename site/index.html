Email marketing, without the platform.

mailgoose does three things: it helps you design an email, sends it to the people who asked to hear from you, and collects new subscribers with signup forms. Everything you can do in the web app, you can also do from a command line, so the AI agent you already use can do it too.

Just the five things you need

No CRM, no ad manager, no website builder. A short list of features that work well.

  • Email designer

    Drag and drop blocks, or bring your own HTML and edit it with a live preview.

  • Contacts and tags

    One audience per account, organized with tags. Import a CSV or add people by hand.

  • Campaigns

    Send a test, check it, then confirm to send it to everyone you picked.

  • Signup forms

    Copy an embed code onto your site. New subscribers confirm by email before they're added.

  • Reports

    Deliveries, opens, clicks, bounces and unsubscribes for each campaign.

  • A CLI for your agent

    Every action in the app has a command, so Claude, Codex or any agent can build and send for you.

Built for your AI agent, too

The CLI covers everything the web app does. An agent can build an email block by block and send you a test, but a campaign only goes out after you confirm it with a one-time code.

# sign in once
mailgoose login

# build an email from blocks, then check it
mailgoose email create
mailgoose email blocks add
mailgoose email check

# test it, then launch with your confirmation
mailgoose campaign test
mailgoose campaign launch

How we keep email wanted

mailgoose sends marketing email from our customers to their own subscribers: people who signed up on the customer's site or asked to hear from them. Every account agrees to our anti-spam policy, and the app enforces it.

  • Only people who opted in

    Bought, rented, shared and scraped lists aren't allowed. Signup forms use double opt-in by default, so each new subscriber confirms by email first.

  • Unsubscribe in every email

    mailgoose adds a working unsubscribe link and one-click unsubscribe header to every campaign, and honors them at once. Every email also carries the sender's postal address.

  • Bounces and complaints handled

    A hard bounce or a spam complaint stops all future mail to that address straight away. Repeated soft bounces do the same.

  • Accounts that misbehave are paused

    We watch each account's bounce and complaint rates and pause its sending when they climb, before they can hurt anyone else's delivery.

  • New accounts are reviewed

    We review new accounts by hand during the beta, and new accounts can only send to their own test addresses until we allow more.

  • Imports are checked

    Imported lists with signs of being bought or scraped are held for review before anyone on them gets email.
